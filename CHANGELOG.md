# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section becomes the release notes and the "What's new" window in the launcher.

## 1.0.0

- New voxel cosmetics: 56 items and 10 full costumes (wings, helmets, masks, horns, crowns, armour, weapons).
- Costume sets in the wardrobe: flip through them with the arrows and wear a whole set in one click.
- New Weapons slot, armour now covers arms and legs.
- After a crash the launcher names mods that do not work together and offers to disable one.
- Known incompatible mods are flagged: Iris with Embeddium, Oculus or Rubidium, Sodium with Embeddium, Krypton with e4mc.
- Warning when mods change while the game is running, with a Restart button.
- Shader settings files (.txt) no longer show up in the shader list.
- Fixed the launch panel overflowing on narrow windows.
